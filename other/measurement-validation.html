1. Financial Performance
6 metrics: Combined ratio (87.3% baseline), loss ratio (64%), expense ratio (23.3%), cost per claim, premium growth, ROI. Market model projects operational metrics → EPS, stock price, market cap.
2. Operational Performance
12 metrics: Cycle time, exception rate, first-pass accuracy, supplement rate, translation debt index, handoff failure rate, evidence completeness, interruption readiness, agent authority width, and 4 SWT-specific operational metrics.
3. Organizational Health
9 metrics: Employee AI trust, trust polarization, claims adjuster attrition, learning loop closure rate, pattern reuse rate, informal control points surfaced, playbook growth, change exhaustion trend.
4. Customer Impact
4 metrics: Customer retention after claim (proxy), CSAT (directional), claim error rate, time to first contact.
5. Competitive Position
4 metrics: Market share, competitive absorption speed, pricing accuracy, Robinsons segment growth. Benchmarked against GEICO (91.2 CR), Allstate (86.6), industry (92.0).
6. Governance & Risk
6 metrics: Regulatory findings, AI bias incidents, audit trail completeness, intervention response time, model drift events, stop button testability.

What Proof Looks Like

1. The simulation surprises us
Novelty in the novelty register is the single best signal. If every output confirms what we already believe, we built an echo chamber.
2. Practitioners recognize the behavior
If a claims VP reads Diana's decision log and says "I've managed three of her," the model captures something real. Face validity from domain experts.
3. Results are robust, not fragile
Run 5 times with different seeds. Specific discoveries vary. Structural patterns hold. Small parameter changes don't flip the results.
4. Track B isn't a straw man
Track B produces SOME improvement. Steering committee makes SOME good decisions. If Track B is a punching bag, the experiment is propaganda.
5. The counterfactual is honest
Can we construct conditions where Track B outperforms? If political friction is low, Diana cooperates, existing process is clean — traditional change management might win.
