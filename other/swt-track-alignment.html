Operating model is DISCOVERED, not designed

Value anchoring: attach AI to a business lever

Deploy into existing structures — no reorg before evidence
DeployWithoutReorganizing constraint: no new roles, no reporting line changes, no RACI redesign. AI gets access to same systems the human role has. Output enters at the same handoff point. Existing roles interact with it using existing authority.
Source: "The AI Operating Model Is Discovered, Not Designed," "You Are Not Deploying Agents"

LearningLoop captures both outputs. Playbook accumulates patterns. Governance updates harden what worked. Decision frameworks make judgment reusable. Next deployment starts from evidence, not opinion.

SWT's Specific Concerns — How We Measure Them

Translation Debt
translation_debt_index: handoff failures + reconciliation incidents per 100 claims. handoff_failure_rate: % of claims with at least one translation incident. Declining over time = seams are being redesigned.
Bottleneck Migration
Bottleneck detection each sprint: which step is the constraint? Tracked across sprints. At least 2 migrations expected in 16 sprints. Stagnant = not enough deployment cycles.
Exception Inflation
exception_rate: % of decisions requiring human intervention beyond designed process. Initial spike after deployment, then decline as seams are redesigned. Flatline at zero = agents aren't producing realistic friction.
Interruption Readiness
interruption_readiness: 0-1. Can named individuals stop/narrow/override AI within one business day? Designed after first deployment. Autonomy widens ONLY after interruption is designed.
Evidence Completeness
evidence_completeness: % of AI decisions with full audit trail (what, why, authority, inputs, changes, escalation). SWT: "the receipt is the product."
Agent Authority Width
agent_authority_width: dollar threshold for autonomous AI decisions. Should widen with evidence. Starts at $5K, widens to $15K+ as interruption is proven. Widening without evidence = deferred risk.
Learning Velocity
learning_loop_closure_rate: discoveries → redesign within 2 sprints. pattern_reuse_rate: later deployments informed by earlier evidence. Flat = learning isn't compounding.
Absence Detection
AbsenceDetector: surfaces decisions made from incomplete information. Stale data, missing pauses, unrecognized gaps. Type 2 emergence — the system didn't know what it didn't know.

Six Gaps We Filled

SWT's framework implies these capabilities but doesn't specify them operationally. We made them explicit so the simulation can implement them. These need validation.

Gap 1 — Interruption Design
Implementation: After first deployment, explicitly design who can pause, override, and stop the AI. Sanjay can pause, Diana can pause individual decisions, Kathryn can stop the entire deployment. Interruption is designed, not improvised.
Gap 2 — Translation Capability Evaluation
Implementation: After each redesign, score the workflow on SWT's four translation capabilities: structure extraction, meaning portability, routing accuracy, throttling. Scored from observation data, not hardcoded.
Gap 3 — Evidence, Receipts & Decision Dockets
Implementation: Every AI decision produces a structured audit trail as part of the workflow itself. Tracked via evidence_completeness metric. Not an after-the-fact compliance exercise.
Gap 4 — Learning Extraction Failure Modes
Implementation: Learning extraction can fail. Busyness (stress > threshold → 50% chance of missed capture). Politics (Diana suppresses discoveries that threaten her informal authority). Recognition (low AI literacy → patterns not recognized).
Gap 5 — Stop & Adapt Logic
Implementation: Explicit stop criteria evaluated every 2 sprints. No measurable business impact within 3 sprints → stop and redirect. Not failure — data. Move to next opportunity.
Gap 6 — Value Anchoring vs Use-Case Brainstorming
Implementation: Track A's first decision: "which business lever needs improvement?" → then ask whether AI helps. Track B's first decision: "where can we use AI?" → then connect to business lever. This is the fork in sprint 1.

What We Do NOT Do (Track A)

No steering committee
Decisions made by accountable owner with bounded authority, not by monthly committee reviewing dashboards.
No future-state design
No target operating model designed upfront. No new roles created before evidence justifies them.
No adoption metrics
License activation, training completion, AI-assisted claim % — these are activity, not outcome. Track B measures them. Track A doesn't.
No vendor platform
No RFP process. No enterprise platform selection. AI capability is deployed into the existing tech stack within the bounded scope.
