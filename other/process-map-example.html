What this map exposes

  • The handoff from sales rep to attorney is where things break -- no triage, no self-serve option
  • Steps 3 and 4 are waste: the attorney is doing lookup work, not legal judgment
  • Step 5 is waste: the attorney is retyping information that already exists in a document
3 of 5 steps are low-value for the attorney. Actual legal skill is not being used.
