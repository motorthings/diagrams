Executive Summary

One approach discovered what worked. The other announced what would work.

Track A — the organic, evidence-driven method — produced better outcomes on 5 of 6 metrics that showed meaningful divergence. Lower costs. Fewer errors. More customers stayed. But the real story isn't the score. It's the mechanism.

Track A won 5 of 6 metrics that showed meaningful divergence. "Winner" means better business results, not better feelings.

The real difference: Track A found 6 real friction patterns and validated each one with evidence before acting. Track B's discoveries auto-advanced on schedule — same number of findings, zero evidence required. One track let reality correct the plan. The other defended the plan from reality. That's why Track A's employees were more skeptical of AI but produced better results with it. Skepticism plus evidence beats enthusiasm plus PowerPoint.

Bottom line: If you're leading an AI transformation, stop asking 'what's our adoption target?' and start asking 'what just broke, and what did we learn?' The operating model isn't something you design in a conference room. It's something you discover through implementation — one bounded deployment at a time.

Why They Differ — The Rules

Interruption Design
Track A: Pause, override, and stop are designed into the workflow. Agents exercise them without political consequence. Result: more exceptions surfaced, more supplements requested.
Track B: Exceptions are escalations — they signal failure. Governance filters before visibility. Result: fewer exceptions surfaced, but handoff failures accumulate.
Learning Extraction
Track A: After every deployment, the Discovery Engine finds patterns, proposes ONE redesign. Translation debt is addressed as it appears.
Track B: Learning goes through steering committee → roadmap phase → vendor assessment. Translation debt accumulates until the "consolidate gains" phase.
Deployment Constraint
Track A: Deploy AI into EXISTING structures. No reorg, no new roles, no RACI redesign. The operating model is discovered through implementation.
Track B: Reorg first (McKinsey 7S), train first (ADKAR), build coalition first (Kotter Step 2). The operating model is designed before implementation.
THEARI Gating
Track A: Evidence-gated. A discovery only advances when it has enough evidence sprints + confidence + measured improvement. No auto-advance.
Track B: Timeline-gated. Discoveries auto-advance after N sprints regardless of evidence. Theoretical → Empirical in 4 sprints, Empirical → Applicable in 5.
