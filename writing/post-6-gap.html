Post 6

The Gap

Names the category. Introduces OS.

The thesis

Between the kickoff speech and a portfolio of governed, evaluated, compound-improving agents, there is nothing. No shared methodology. No compounding knowledge. Every team invents its own approach. Three different platforms. Three different definitions of done. The Legal team's learnings never reach Finance. Every agent is a one-off. Every failure is private.

Parallel isolation, running faster.

The technology gets cheaper every quarter. The models commoditize. The moat is not the AI. The moat is the operating system around it — methodology, evaluation, repair loops, portfolio visibility, decision architecture.

Nobody is selling that layer. The studios sold a text box with a logo. The enterprise needs an operating system.

AESOP Transformation OS enters here

Not as a product pitch. As the logical answer to a diagnosed gap. Eleven pipeline stages. Six evaluation modes. A portfolio layer above them. Every stage built backward from a failure. Every gate placed where fluency tried to talk its way past a human decision.

Why this post is sixth

  • The transition — after five posts of diagnosis, this names what's missing
  • OS enters as category need first, specific solution second
  • "Nobody is selling that layer" — market gap, not product announcement
  • Closes the buffet metaphor from Post 2: "Nobody sold the kitchen"
  • Promise to walk through architecture — sets up Posts 7 and 8
  • Hiring managers see market insight. Consultants see a lever they could use
