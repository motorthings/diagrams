# Before the Session

This is a 30-minute session. You don't need to install anything or prepare anything technical.

Here's what will happen: Charlie is going to work through a real project in front of you. Meeting transcripts, action items, Jira tickets, discovery notes, a PRD — you'll see how they connect and where AI fits in each transition. The goal is not to explain how it works. It's to show you.

Watch what gets handed to Claude. Watch where Charlie makes the call himself. That gap — synthesis on one side, judgment on the other — is what this course is about.

---

## How this course works

This is not a course you plow through. It is a course you do in stages, then stop and talk.

The structure is: complete a stage, try it in your real work for a week or two, then check in with Charlie and the group. What did you actually do? What worked? What felt awkward? What surprised you? That conversation is not a debrief — it shapes what happens next.

Charlie is running this with a small group of people at the same time. Everyone's role is different. Everyone will use this differently. The patterns that emerge across all of you — what clicks, what doesn't, where people get stuck — inform how the next stage gets built. Your experience is not just useful to you. It feeds back into the course itself.

So the job is not to get through the material. It is to get into your work, try things, and come back with something real to say about it. That is how the course gets better for everyone who comes after you.

---

## One thing to bring

A friction point from your actual work. Something you do repeatedly that takes longer than it should. You don't need to share it. Just have it in mind. By the end of the session you will probably have a sense of whether there is a path to fixing it.

---

## After the session

You will start Stage 1. It is short — a folder, a few files, one configuration. The session will make clear why it is the right starting point.

Use it for a week or two in your actual work. Then come back.
