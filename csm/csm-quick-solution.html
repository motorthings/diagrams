The Quick solution, mapped.

How Amazon Quick turns sources into actions — and the four gaps Quick doesn’t fill. The gaps are the job.

Source (data) Quick platform (decide + sequence) Agent + gate (narrate + gate) Action (write / notify) Gap — you build
01 Sources — what Quick ingests

Salesforce

The CRM. Both a source (account state) and the write target the agent updates.

Totango

Customer success. The risk signals — adoption, health, touchpoints.

Redshift

The analytics warehouse — historical, queryable, aggregated.

S3

Raw landing zone — extracts and logs before anything is modeled.

SharePoint · Confluence

Documents and knowledge — playbooks, runbooks, the narrative context.

Telemetry

Product usage — the behavioral signal the model reads.

02 Actions — what the agent’s hands do

Write to Salesforce

Update the record. This is the write-back — the action a draft alone can’t do.

Create a ticket

Jira, ServiceNow — the 50+ built-in actions. Fires a workflow, not a message.

Notify a human

Slack alert. The hand-off when something needs judgment.

Gate

Every write passes a human confirmation. Quick’s per-action allow/deny and HITL on write actions is the built-in gate — the platform’s version of “human above the loop.”

03 The gaps — what you build

ID reconciliation

Six sources, no shared account ID. Quick ingests them but won’t reconcile them. Unsafe to act until it does.

Deterministic scoring

The risk math. Code decides, not the model — Quick narrates but doesn’t compute the score.

Grounding · eval

Does the draft cite a real signal? Quick cites sources for retrieval, but not that the claim traces to the score.

Outcome measurement

Did the action move revenue, not hours? Quick tracks nothing about renewal or retention.

Through-line

Quick orchestrates and gates. You supply the scoring and the eval. Risk math is code, the model narrates, the human gates — and the outcome is measured outside the platform.

04 How it wires

sync → ingest → context

Six sources feed the connectors, land in the index, become the agent’s retrieval context.

plan → draft → approve

The orchestrator sequences, the agent drafts, the human gate holds the write until confirmed.

approve → execute

The gated action fires — write, ticket, or notify — and the gap you build measures the outcome.
