Legal AI Functions

Two standalone applications already built. One clear extension path. An architecture that maps directly to what a law firm K&I team needs to deliver — without coupling to any single platform.

Each legal AI function stands on its own. The Matter Intake Evaluator classifies, scores, and routes incoming matters. The Contract Review application handles vendor, customer, and employment agreements. Each owns its UI, its workflow, and its data. No shared database. No platform lock-in.

The shared pattern is governance. Every function exposes the same three endpoints — health, metrics, and evaluation target. A thin governance layer polls them for portfolio tracking, agent evaluation, and adoption monitoring. The functions don't depend on each other. The governance layer doesn't depend on any one function.

The extension is straightforward: add legal-domain functions — matter intake, diligence, regulatory monitoring, knowledge management, client reporting — each with its own evaluation hooks. Same integration contract, independent surface area.

JD Requirement → What's Already True

The K&I Program Manager role at Ashurst Perkins Coie asks for someone who bridges legal practice, technology, and business operations. That bridge exists. Here's the map.

Partner with legal teams to identify AI opportunities
Done. Embedded in a large corporate legal department: stakeholder listening sessions, workflow mapping, structured requirements before any build. The legal function roadmap above came from the same discovery pattern.
Translate legal needs into AI solution requirements; facilitate POC development
Done, twice. Contract Review: 5 agent types mapped to specific contract categories with domain-tailored criteria. Employment Legal: separation agreement agent, legal metrics agent — each scoped to a specific legal workflow.
Design and deploy scalable AI tools with K&I, data, e-discovery, and technology teams
Contract Review runs on Vercel + Railway + Supabase + Celery/Redis. 360 contracts/hour throughput. Built for production, not demo. Multi-format ingestion, RAG pipeline, row-level security — infrastructure that scales across functions.
Ensure AI solutions align with governance, ethics, and risk standards
The evaluation framework is the governance layer. Multi-agent scoring with hard veto rules. Regulatory mapping mode generates compliance artifacts from evaluation history. The enterprise employment legal work required confidentiality walls between employment and commercial legal — the access-control architecture encodes that pattern.
Lead end-to-end delivery of AI-enabled client projects — concept through implementation
The delivery pipeline (Discover → Design → Build → Evaluate → Deploy → Monitor → Repair) is the standard framework. Every function follows it. For the K&I context, this means each new legal AI initiative has a repeatable path, not a reinvention.
Develop frameworks to track AI solution performance — time saved, cost impact, quality
The portfolio layer tracks every agent: cost, status, outcomes. Contract Review dashboard adds KPI cards, risk score trends, throughput analytics. The framework exists — it just needs legal-domain metrics plugged in.
Maintain a portfolio dashboard — adoption, client outcomes, return on innovation
The portfolio dashboard is cross-function by design. A K&I director would see every AI investment — contract review, matter intake, diligence — in one view with adoption rates and outcome metrics.
Serve as strategic advisor — position AI as value-added differentiator
Client Value Reporting function (roadmap) generates outcome artifacts: "here's what AI saved this quarter." The whole platform is a client-conversation asset. And the competitive research doc in the Contract Review repo demonstrates fluency in the legal AI market (Ironclad, LawGeex, Kira, Harvey, Spellbook).
Partner with L&D to upskill lawyers and staff in AI literacy
This is the deepest lane. 150+ professionals trained monthly. Enterprise AI literacy workshops for legal, finance, and people ops teams. The operationalization layer auto-generates training and adoption plans per agent.
Promote a culture of experimentation, measurement, and continuous improvement
The feedback loop in Contract Review turns individual reviewer corrections into systematic improvements. Drift detection monitors agent performance over time. The whole architecture is built on the premise that shipping is step one — the system gets smarter with use.
