Legal AI Project Registry

Four legal AI projects — scoped, stakeholder-validated, and built or designed inside a large corporate legal department. Each with distinct governance demands. One pattern across all of them.

Projects — Enterprise Legal Portfolio
Built Employment Law
Separation Agreement & Severance Calculator
Generates jurisdiction-specific separation agreements across US, EMEA, and Australia. Calculates severance based on country and length of service. Validates proposed amounts against policy and flags discrepancies. Processes ~100 agreements/year — the team's highest-priority automation target.
Maximum confidentiality Ethical wall required Multi-jurisdiction Audit trail
Stakeholders Employment Legal Lead, EMEA Counsel, US/AU Counsel, Legal Ops
Built Legal Operations
Legal Metrics Analysis Agent
Natural-language query agent over legal operational data. Outside counsel spend by firm and practice area. Procurement and revenue-contract execution volumes. Replaced manual pulls across Tableau, spreadsheets, and Brightflag. Restricted to Legal Leadership and Legal Ops only.
Restricted distribution Multi-source data Monthly refresh Natural language
Stakeholders Legal Ops, Legal Leadership Team
Prototyped Corporate Law
State Annual Report Filing Agent
Automates Secretary of State annual report filings across all registered states. Same core data (officers, addresses, tax status) into slightly different state forms. Low stakes, high repetition — a quick-win agent for the corporate team. Failure to file = loss of good standing.
Template population Filing status tracking Deadline awareness
Stakeholders Employment Legal Lead, Corporate team
Configured Commercial Contracts
Cowork Legal Plugin — Vendor Contract Review
Anthropic legal plugin configured with the firm's negotiation playbook. Clause-by-clause analysis with GREEN/YELLOW/RED classification. NDA triage, DPA compliance review, redline generation. Three coordinated plugins (Legal, Procurement, Operations) chained into a single review workflow.
Playbook-driven Plugin chain Red / Yellow / Green Risk classification Redline generation
Stakeholders Legal Counsel, Legal Ops

The Pattern — What All Four Share
4/4
Started with stakeholder discovery — "go in to learn, not tell"
4/4
Access control defined before build — who can see what, enforced structurally
4/4
Human decision point identified — AI recommends, human decides
3/4
Multi-jurisdiction or multi-source data complexity

Why this matters for a law firm K&I role: These aren't demos. Each project went through the full cycle — stakeholder listening session, scoped PRD, governance requirements defined, access control architected, build, deploy. The Separation Agreement agent is the hardest one: maximum confidentiality, multi-jurisdiction, employment law sensitivity, ethical wall requirement. If you can govern that, you can govern any legal AI function.

The governance connection: Every governance requirement in the map above — access control, audit trail, human-in-the-loop, model evaluation, jurisdictional variation — is a capability the evaluation framework already provides. These four projects are the evidence that the pattern works across the full legal domain surface: employment, corporate, operations, and commercial contracts.
