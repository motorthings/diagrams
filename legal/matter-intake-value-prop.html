Why Matter Intake
Evaluation Matters

The strategic case for AI-powered intake as the highest-leverage entry point in any law firm.

01

Intake is the bottleneck

Every new client engagement starts here. Conflicts, staffing, risk appetite, billing arrangements — all decided in the first hours of intake. Getting it wrong means malpractice exposure, profitability loss, or missed business. Getting it right, consistently, is a structural competitive advantage.

02

The perfect AI wedge

Intake evaluation is structured enough for reliable AI (classification + scoring across known dimensions) but nuanced enough to need LLM reasoning (conflict patterns, risk judgment, staffing logic). It's not a toy demo — it's a production-grade triage tool that demonstrably reduces the time from inquiry to engagement letter.

03

Auditability is the differentiator

Every AmLaw firm evaluating AI asks the same question: "Can we trust it?" The audit trail architecture answers that. Not "AI says X" — but "here's exactly why, with raw data to prove it, and an immutable record." Every prompt, every response, every rubric version captured. Reproducible. Explainable. Defensible.

04

It scales

Start with intake evaluation. The same two-stage pipeline + audit trail architecture extends to conflict waivers, engagement letters, staffing optimization, matter budgeting, and lateral hiring diligence. The pattern is the platform — not a point solution, but a capability.

The strategic thesis

Law firms don't compete on legal reasoning — every AmLaw 50 firm has brilliant lawyers. They compete on operational excellence at scale. The firm that can evaluate, triage, and staff new matters faster and more consistently than its peers wins the margin game.

AI-augmented intake doesn't replace lawyer judgment — it elevates it. It handles the structured classification and risk-flagging so partners can focus on the judgment calls that actually need their expertise. The result: faster time-to-engagement, fewer missed conflicts, more consistent staffing, and a complete audit trail for every decision.

For Perkins Coie specifically — a firm with a strong technology practice and an established innovation culture — this isn't a stretch proposition. It's an accelerant on a trajectory they're already on.
