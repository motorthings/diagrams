RED FLAG: Legal Agents — Resume Overstates Build Status
This is the most important gap to fix before the recruiter screen.

Separation agent: Resume says "Designed." PRD says "Status: Discovery." The PRD IS thorough design work — defensible to say "designed." But not built.

Legal metrics agent: Resume says "Built." PRD says "Status: Ready for Build." This is the biggest mismatch. "Ready for Build" means requirements complete, build was next. "Built" implies deployed. Fix: change resume language to "scoped and validated requirements with Legal Ops" or confirm whether a Glean agent was actually deployed.

State reports: Resume says "Scoped and prototyped." PRD says "Status: Discovery." "Scoped" is accurate. "Prototyped" is not supported by the PRD.

Honest framing for the interview: "I ran four legal AI discovery engagements. One — contract review — I built end-to-end as a standalone application. The separation agreement agent was fully designed and stakeholder-validated. The legal metrics agent had complete requirements and was next in the build queue. The pattern across all four is what matters for this role: discovery-first, access control architected before build, human decision point in every workflow."
