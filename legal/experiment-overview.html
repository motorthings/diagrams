What Actually Happens When AI Hits a Law Firm

two identical firms · one discovers its new operating model, one designs it on paper · and what that does to the billable hour, the redline, and the leverage pyramid

The Question

A law firm runs on a simple machine: associates bill the hours that pay the partners. AI can now draft the motions, run the research, review the documents. So the real question isn't which tool to buy — it's can a firm change how it works — discovered through the work itself, or designed on paper first?

Track A

SWT Organic Discovery

Stuart Winter-Tear thesis · implement, observe, then redesign
  • Deploy AI into one bounded workflow — drafting assistance for motion practice
  • No reorganization up front
  • Observe what breaks for 2 sprints
  • Extract learning from what breaks
  • Redesign ONE seam based on evidence
