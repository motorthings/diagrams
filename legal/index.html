The Boundary

The platform recommends. A licensed attorney decides.

I am a non-JD legal engineer. I know exactly where the software stops and the lawyer begins, and I design to that line deliberately. My systems supervise AI assistance the way the rules require: automated output is screened, routed, and escalated to an attorney who holds the decision.

The UPL line is not a disclaimer. It is the discipline that makes the tools safe enough to ship and honest enough to stand behind.
