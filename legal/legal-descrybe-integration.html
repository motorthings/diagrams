Descrybe Integration

Legal research stops being a thing you go do. It becomes a thing that happens before you look.

Executive Summary plain english +
What we built with Descrybe
  • Descrybe is now the research engine of Legal AI OS. Not a bolt-on tool. Not a side panel. The platform’s research layer.
  • Every new matter arrives with Descrybe’s case law and statutes already surfaced — matched to its jurisdiction and practice area — before anyone reviews it. The research is finished before a human thinks to ask for it.
  • Citation checking that actually checks. Drop in a cite and the exact wording, and Descrybe confirms the quote appears where you say it does. No hallucinated cases. No garbled cites.
Why Descrybe makes the platform stronger
  • Structured primary law, not a flat keyword search. Real, resolvable authorities a lawyer can stand behind.
  • Research happens before review, not after. The platform hands a lawyer a brief, not a blank page.
  • Everything Descrybe returns lands on the permanent audit trail. When the client or the court asks “why did you cite this,” the answer is one query away.

Two paths in, one audited core. Auto-enrichment on matter creation. On-demand research and citation checks from the attorney or any function. Every result cached, every call written to the immutable audit trail.

What got built

Four pieces. Each one fits the existing governance contract.

The research connection

Links the platform to Descrybe. Searches case law, statutes, and regulations, then brings results back in one consistent form. Each public-law question is answered once and reused — no repeat work.

Automatic matter research

Turns what a matter already knows — its jurisdiction, practice area, and the parties involved — into a set of searches. Runs before anyone reviews it. Never slows the intake down.

Three things a lawyer can do

Run a research query. Verify a citation. Enrich an existing matter. Registered as the platform’s ninth function.

A permanent record

Keeps track of every query and every result. Public law is shared and reused; the matter’s private details stay private.

What you get

Not a research tool bolted on the side. Research woven into the workflow.

Research before review

Matters arrive with authority

A new matter lands with controlling case law and governing statutes already surfaced from its own jurisdiction and practice area. The partner reviews a brief, not a blank page.

Citations that check themselves

Verify the quote, not just the cite

Drop a citation and the exact language. The system resolves it against Descrybe's corpus and tells you whether the words actually appear where you say they do.

Every query provable

The audit trail is the product

Every research call is captured — what was asked, what came back, what it cost. When the client asks why you cited something, the answer is one query away.
