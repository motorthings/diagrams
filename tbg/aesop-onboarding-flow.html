04 Day-to-Day
Your Workflow
  1. TBG sends link. Paige or Aparna emails the intake URL to a new client before their coaching engagement.
  2. Client fills form. ~20 minutes. Privacy disclosure first, then 29 questions. Every field optional except business name.
  3. AESOP generates. ~2 minutes in background. Client sees confirmation page.
  4. Charlie gets notified. Email arrives with direct link to results page. Charlie reviews the roadmap, downloads the PDF.
  5. Charlie syncs to TBG. Files PDF in TBG > Clients > [Client Name] > AI Adoption Roadmap.pdf. Notifies Paige / Aparna.
  6. TBG preps for 1-on-1. Review the roadmap before the session. The roadmap is a discussion starter — coaching makes it actionable.
If the Client Loses Their Link

Find their submission in the admin dashboard at /admin/platform (Intake Submissions tab). Copy the token URL: https://aesopstudio.com/executive-intake/results/{token}. Send it to the client.

If Something Goes Wrong

Roadmap didn't generate? Check admin dashboard — status may be failed. Charlie investigates.

Client wants data deleted? Forward to Charlie or email privacy@waifinder.org. Deleted within 7 business days.
