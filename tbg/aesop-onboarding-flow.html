04 Day-to-Day
Your Workflow
  1. Send link. Email the intake URL to a new client before their coaching engagement.
  2. Wait for notification. Charlie forwards the "Roadmap Ready" email when it arrives (or you get it directly once admin access is set up).
  3. Open results page. Click the link in the email. Review the roadmap — maturity model, strategic themes, phased projects.
  4. Download PDF. Use the download button on the results page.
  5. File in Drive. Save to TBG > Clients > [Client Name] > AI Adoption Roadmap.pdf.
  6. Prep for 1-on-1. Review before the session. The roadmap is a discussion starter — your coaching makes it actionable.
If the Client Loses Their Link

Find their submission in the admin dashboard at /admin/platform (Intake Submissions tab). Copy the token URL: https://aesopstudio.com/executive-intake/results/{token}. Send it to the client.

If Something Goes Wrong

Roadmap didn't generate? Check admin dashboard — status may be failed. Charlie investigates.

Client wants data deleted? Forward to Charlie or email privacy@waifinder.org. Deleted within 7 business days.
