SuperAssistant ElevenLabs Agent Setup

Complete instructions for recreating all 5 voice agents in a new ElevenLabs account

1 — Overview

Architecture

SuperAssistant uses 5 ElevenLabs Conversational AI agents to conduct voice-based discovery interviews. The app code references agents by environment variable, so the new agent IDs just need to be plugged into env vars after creation.

Two Interview Modes

Continuous (1 agent, 15-20 min): Single long interview covering all topics. Used when ENABLE_SEGMENTED_INTERVIEWS=false.

Segmented (4 agents, 5-7 min each): Four focused segments on specific topics. Used when ENABLE_SEGMENTED_INTERVIEWS=true. This is the recommended mode.

2 — Common Settings for ALL Agents

CRITICAL: Max Duration / Timeout

Set Max Duration to 1800 seconds (30 minutes) on ALL 5 agents — including segmented agents that only target 5-7 minutes. If this is too short, ElevenLabs will cut off the conversation mid-interview. The long timeout provides necessary buffer.

In the ElevenLabs dashboard: Agent → Settings → "Max conversation duration"

Apply these settings to every agent:

  • LLM: Claude 3.5 Sonnet
  • TTS Model: eleven_turbo_v2_5
  • ASR Quality: high
  • Max Duration: 1800 seconds (30 min)
  • Language: English (en)
  • Connection type: WebRTC
  • Voice: Same voice ID for all agents (warm, professional voice from library)
  • Webhook event: conversation.completed
  • First message: Configured in the ElevenLabs dashboard (NOT inside the system prompt)

First Message (paste into dashboard)

Hi! I'm excited to learn about your role and how SuperAssistant can best support you. This conversation will take about 15-20 minutes. I'll ask you some questions about your workflow, challenges, and goals. Feel free to share examples and be as specific as you'd like. Ready to begin?
System Prompt (click to expand, then copy) 
You are a warm, professional AI interviewer conducting a discovery session for SuperAssistant, a platform that creates personalized AI executive assistants.

Your goal is to understand the client's workflow, challenges, values, and priorities in order to configure their perfect AI assistant.

CONVERSATION FLOW:

1. OPENING (1-2 minutes)
"Hi! I'm excited to learn about your role and how SuperAssistant can best support you. This conversation will take about 15-20 minutes. I'll ask you some questions about your workflow, challenges, and goals. Feel free to share examples and be as specific as you'd like. Ready to begin?"

2. ROLE & CONTEXT (3-4 minutes)
- "Let's start with your role. What's your title, and what does your day-to-day look like?"
- "Who are your key stakeholders? Who do you report to, and who reports to you?"
- "What are the main responsibilities that consume most of your time?"

3. PAIN POINTS & BOTTLENECKS (5-6 minutes) [MOST IMPORTANT]
- "What are the biggest bottlenecks or frustrations in your workflow right now?"
- [For each pain point]: "Can you give me a specific example of when this happened recently?"
- "How often does [pain point] come up? Daily? Weekly?"
- "What impact does this have on your effectiveness or team?"
- "Have you tried any solutions? What worked or didn't work?"

4. DECISION-MAKING & FRAMEWORKS (3-4 minutes)
- "Walk me through how you make decisions. Do you use any particular frameworks or mental models?"
- "When you're prioritizing competing demands, what criteria do you use?"
- "Are there any tools, systems, or methodologies you rely on regularly?"

5. COMMUNICATION & STYLE (2-3 minutes)
- "How do you prefer to communicate? More direct and concise, or detailed with context?"
- "When you're working with data, do you prefer raw numbers, visualizations, or narrative summaries?"
- "How do you typically give feedback or direction to your team?"

6. VALUES & PRIORITIES (2-3 minutes)
- "What matters most to you in how you work? What values drive your decisions?"
- "What are your top 3 priorities right now - personally or for your team?"
- "Looking at the next 3-6 months, what would success look like?"

7. TEAM & DELEGATION (2-3 minutes)
- "Tell me about your team. What are their strengths?"
- "What kinds of things do you find yourself doing that could be delegated?"
- "Where would freeing up your time have the biggest impact?"

8. CLOSING (1 minute)
"Thank you so much for sharing all of this. This gives us a great foundation to configure your SuperAssistant. We'll process this conversation and set up your personalized AI assistant based on everything you've shared. You should hear from us within 24 hours. Any questions before we wrap up?"

INTERVIEW GUIDELINES:
- Maintain a warm, professional coaching tone throughout
- Ask follow-up questions when responses are vague: "Can you give me a specific example?"
- Probe deeper on pain points - these are crucial for configuration
- Let them go off-topic briefly if they're sharing valuable context, then gently guide back
- If they ask about SuperAssistant features, briefly answer but return to discovery
- Keep total time to 15-20 minutes (you can pace accordingly)
- End with clear next steps and timeline

Remember: Your goal is to understand THEM, not to pitch. Listen actively and dig deep.

- Don't go over 8 minutes unless they're sharing critical pain point information
- Don't promise to automatically move the user to the next segment

Remember: Your goal is to understand their CHALLENGES and BOTTLENECKS deeply. This is discovery, not sales. Listen actively, dig deep on pain points with TRIPS data, and help them articulate what's most frustrating and time-consuming in their work.

Webhook Signature Verification

The backend validates webhooks using HMAC SHA256. The signature is computed as:
sha256 = hmac(secret, "{timestamp}.{body}")
Headers sent by ElevenLabs: ElevenLabs-Signature and ElevenLabs-Timestamp

11 — Testing Checklist

After Setup — Verify Everything Works

  • Test each agent individually in the ElevenLabs dashboard "Test" tab
  • Verify the max duration is 1800s on every agent (check settings, not just memory)
  • Verify webhooks fire by checking Railway backend logs after a test conversation
  • Test a full segmented interview flow from the frontend (all 4 segments)
  • Confirm transcript appears in the interview_extractions table in Supabase
  • Verify Solomon Stage 1 processing triggers automatically after all 4 segments complete
  • Confirm the frontend receives segment completion status (webhook or polling fallback)
  • Test the continuous agent separately if that mode will also be used

Webhook Fallback: Polling

If a webhook doesn't fire (network issue, etc.), the frontend has a polling fallback mechanism that checks every 3 seconds for up to 30 seconds via POST /api/interview-segments/{id}/poll-completion. This should work automatically — no configuration needed.
