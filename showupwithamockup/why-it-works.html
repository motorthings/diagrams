Show Up With A Mockup

Walk in with the answer already built.

Most interview prep stops at research. You read about the company, form opinions, and walk in with a deck.

Research the room, build the artifact, rehearse the conversation. The work is done before the question is asked.

What changes

The artifact does the talking.

A working build on their stack carries the room. The deck walks through what you made.

The conversation moves.

From “could this work on our stack” to “when do we start.” The work is in front of them.

You leave with an asset.

Build to reuse. The same platform grows across interviews, and every build starts further ahead.
