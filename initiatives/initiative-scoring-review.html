6 -- Key Themes

Theme 1
Infrastructure Systematically Underscored
Evals, Sandbox, and Hire-to-Retire all serve as force multipliers but their strategic alignment (H) doesn't reflect this. These enable the entire program. Without Sandbox, CPQ can't be built. Without Evals, nothing is provable to Finance lead.
Theme 2
Pricing Evaluator is the Biggest Miss
Stakeholder mapped it out in detail with a crawl-walk-run path. "30-40% workload reduction" for commercial alone. Scores comparably to Sales Assist Bot (the current #1). Not on the board at all.
Theme 3
Effort (G) Inconsistent with Evidence
Offer Letter Generator scores G=4 despite unconfirmed AI Platform connectors. Invoice-to-PO Matching scores G=2 despite Revenue Operations lead calling it the easiest win with no blockers. Effort scores don't reflect dependency reality.
Theme 4
Legal Department Underrepresented
Legal team lead's DPA review agents and several items from her Legal Agentic Workflows tracker aren't in the main dashboard despite being well-defined, high-volume, and rated "High" in her own scoring.
Theme 5
AI Evals is the Connective Tissue
Its current score (8.4) doesn't reflect that it's the mechanism for answering Finance lead's central question: "How many hours/dollars has AI saved G&A this quarter?" Every deployed agent's value flows through evals.
