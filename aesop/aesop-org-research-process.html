Context Types — 13 Enterprise / 6 SMB

Discovery
Company Profile
Organizational background injected into every discovery interview.
Discovery + Scoring
Strategic Goals
Injected into discovery and portfolio scoring for strategic alignment.
Build
Brand Guidelines
Injected into build synthesis for tone and voice consistency.
Discovery + Design
Org Chart
Who decides what. Maps authority for Delegation Readiness scoring.
Governance
Governance Policy
Risk tolerance, compliance requirements, decision rights framework.
Evaluation
Evaluation Criteria
Org-specific standards injected into rubric generation and scoring.

Deep Research Pipeline — Enterprise Only

Phase 1
Scope
Define research question, sources, and depth from org context gaps.
Phase 2
Search
Tavily web search + SEC EDGAR for public company data.
Phase 3
Fetch
Retrieve full source documents. Extract structured findings.
Phase 4
Verify
Two independent agents vote. Disagreement → re-examine sources.
Phase 5
Synthesize
Merged findings with provenance. Human reviews before pipeline injection.
Write once, read everywhere. Organization Context is not a stage you complete and leave behind. It is the foundation the pipeline stands on — captured once, versioned, and read continuously by discovery, design, build, evaluation, and operationalization. Human-in-the-loop early and often: every context entry and deep research finding passes through a human review gate before it touches a downstream agent.
