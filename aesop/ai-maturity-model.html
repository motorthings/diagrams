AI MATURITY MODEL

AI Maturity Model

A five-level framework for SMB AI adoption. Each level enables certain things and requires certain things. You cannot skip stages.

Aware → Ready → Capable → Mature → Native  ·  you’re only as mature as your weakest dimension

01

The five levels

1
AI Aware“We know AI is coming, but we haven’t started.”
Enables
  • Learning what AI can and can’t do
  • Spotting high-impact starting points
  • Beginning to capture team knowledge
Requires
  • Curiosity and a willingness to learn
  • An honest assessment of the current state
2
AI Ready“Our knowledge is organized. We’ve run one experiment.”
Enables
  • Running a focused AI experiment
  • AI-assisted drafts of existing work
  • Hands-on AI experience for the team
Requires
  • Documented operational knowledge
  • End-to-end workflow maps
  • Baseline team AI literacy
  • Clear decision authority
3
AI Capable“AI supports specific workflows. We measure results.”
Enables
  • AI in production on core workflows
  • Measurable before / after results
  • A prioritized improvement roadmap
Requires
  • One proven, measured success
  • An AI governance document
  • A consistent evaluation framework
  • A team that can assess AI opportunities
4
AI Mature“AI is integrated into operations. We’re scaling what works.”
Enables
  • Multiple AI-assisted workflows
  • Scaling without a founder bottleneck
  • Technology decisions from experience
Requires
  • Proven ROI on initial projects
  • Governance that is routine, not heroic
  • New team members onboarding onto systems
  • Systems that survive key-person absence
5
AI Native“AI is how we think and operate. Continuous improvement.”
Enables
  • AI as a strategic capability
  • Rapid adaptation to new opportunities
  • Compounding improvement over time
Requires
  • AI embedded in decision-making
  • A continuous measurement culture
  • An organization that learns as fast as the tools improve
02

The bottleneck principle

You’re only as mature as your weakest dimension.

A business with Level 4 processes but Level 1 governance is Level 1. A business with clean, organized data but a team that fears AI is stuck until the fear is addressed. The bottleneck dimension sets your true operating level — everything else is potential waiting on permission.

That is why the model scores across dimensions rather than assigning a single number. The lowest score is the honest one. Fix the weakest link first.

Sample profile — GreenScape Landscaping

A 15-person commercial landscaping company. 12 years in business, $2.5M revenue. The founder runs everything from memory — pricing, scheduling, customer history. Experienced crews, but processes aren’t documented. The knowledge can’t transfer.

What this profile tells us: the crews understand the work (Process Clarity: 3), but the business runs on one person’s memory. Decision Governance and Tool Foundation — both Level 1 — are what keep the company at Level 1. The prescription: Phase 1 is Getting Organized — document what the crews know, map where information lives, establish who decides what. Only then can the company advance to Level 2 and run its first AI experiment.

06

Key principles

1
You can’t skip levels.
Each level creates the conditions for the next. A business that deploys AI agents before organizing its information will fail — not because the AI is bad, but because the foundation doesn’t exist. Level 1 done right makes Level 3 possible.
2
Speed comes from foundation, not shortcuts.
The businesses that move fastest are the ones that invest heavily in Level 1 — documentation, process clarity, governance. What feels slow at the start compounds into speed later. Rushing past the foundation guarantees rework.
3
The bottleneck dimension sets your true level.
A high score in one dimension doesn’t compensate for a low score in another. Fix the weakest link first. That is why the model scores across five dimensions — it tells you exactly what to work on, not just “you’re a 2.”
4
Level 5 is a direction, not a destination.
Most SMBs operate at Level 3–4. AI Native is an aspiration describing continuous improvement, not a checkbox. The goal isn’t to reach Level 5. The goal is to keep moving, keep measuring, and keep the bottleneck dimension from holding everything else back.
