AI Maturity Model

AESOP Transformation OS — a five-level framework for SMB AI adoption

Each level of maturity enables certain things and requires certain things. You cannot skip stages. This model helps SMB founders understand where they stand, what’s possible at their current level, and what must be true before advancing.

2 — The Bottleneck Principle

You’re only as mature as your weakest dimension.

A business with Level 4 processes but Level 1 governance is Level 1. A business with clean, organized data but a team that fears AI is stuck until the fear is addressed. The bottleneck dimension sets your true operating level — everything else is potential waiting on permission.

This is why the maturity model scores across dimensions rather than assigning a single number. The lowest score is the honest one. Fix the weakest link first.

3 — The Five Dimensions

Maturity isn’t a single number — it’s a profile across five dimensions. Each dimension is scored 1–5, corresponding to the five maturity levels. The overall maturity is the lowest score across all dimensions.

4 — Sample Profile: GreenScape Landscaping

A 15-person commercial landscaping company. 12 years in business, $2.5M revenue. The founder runs everything from memory — pricing, scheduling, customer history. The crews are experienced but processes aren’t documented. Overall maturity: Level 1 (bottlenecked by Decision Governance and Tool Foundation). The Process Clarity dimension is stronger because the crews know their jobs — but that knowledge isn’t documented and can’t transfer.

6 — Key Principles
1

You can’t skip levels.

Each level creates the conditions for the next. A business that tries to deploy AI agents before organizing its information will fail — not because the AI is bad, but because the foundation doesn’t exist. Level 1 done right makes Level 3 possible.

2

Speed comes from foundation, not shortcuts.

The businesses that move fastest through the levels are the ones that invest heavily in Level 1 — documentation, process clarity, governance. What feels slow at the start compounds into speed later. Rushing past the foundation guarantees rework.

3

The bottleneck dimension sets your true level.

A high score in one dimension doesn’t compensate for a low score in another. Fix the weakest link first. This is why the model scores across five dimensions — it tells you exactly what to work on, not just “you’re a 2.”

4

Level 5 is a direction, not a destination.

Most SMBs will operate at Level 3–4. Level 5 — AI Native — is an aspiration that describes continuous improvement, not a checkbox. The goal isn’t to reach Level 5. The goal is to keep moving, keep measuring, and keep the bottleneck dimension from holding everything else back.
