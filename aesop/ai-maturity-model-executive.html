AI Maturity Model

Where is your business today — and what does it take to reach the next level?

1

"We know AI is coming, but we haven't started."

You're only as mature as your weakest dimension.

A business with great processes but no governance is stuck at the governance level. A business with clean data but a team that fears AI can't move until the fear is addressed. The bottleneck dimension sets your true level — everything else is potential waiting on permission.

The Five Dimensions
Information Readiness
Is your business knowledge documented and findable?
Process Clarity
Are your workflows mapped? Does everyone know who does what?
Decision Governance
Are approval rules clear? Can decisions happen without you?
Team Capability
Does your team understand AI? Are they curious or afraid?
Tool Foundation
Is your data clean? Do your systems work together?
What This Means For You
1

You can't skip levels.

Each level creates the conditions for the next. Trying to deploy AI before organizing your information will fail — not because the AI is bad, but because the foundation doesn't exist.

2

Speed comes from foundation.

The businesses that move fastest invest heavily in Level 1 — documentation, process clarity, governance. What feels slow at the start compounds into speed later.

3

Fix the weakest link first.

Your overall maturity is your lowest dimension score. The model tells you exactly what to work on — not just "you're a 2."

4

Level 5 is a direction, not a destination.

Most businesses operate at Level 3–4. The goal isn't to reach Level 5 — it's to keep moving, keep measuring, and keep the bottleneck from holding everything back.
