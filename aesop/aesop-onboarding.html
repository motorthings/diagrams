Onboarding Phases

Phase 1
Intake
Client receives a public intake link. No login required. Privacy disclosure before the form. Every field is optional except business name. Executive intake has 29 fields; SMB intake uses the full wizard with all modules.
~20 minutes
Phase 2
Deep Research
Five-stage research pipeline activates: Scope → Search → Fetch → Verify → Synthesize. Pulls company profile, strategic goals, brand guidelines, org chart, governance policy, and evaluation criteria. Enterprise only; SMB uses a lightweight profile.
~2-5 minutes
Phase 3
Organization Profile
Research results are compiled into a structured organization context profile. WORE injection (Write Once Read Everywhere) ensures every downstream agent — discovery, design, build, evaluation — builds against the same ground truth.
Automatic
Phase 4
Roadmap Generation
AESOP generates an AI Adoption Roadmap: maturity model level, strategic themes, phased projects, what to avoid, and conditions for the next phase. Output as an interactive results page and downloadable PDF.
~2 minutes
Phase 5
Admin Review
Platform admin reviews the roadmap via the admin dashboard. Status: pending_review → approved or rejected. Rejected roadmaps go to the revision queue for regeneration. Confidence score indicates data completeness and coherence.
Manual gate
Phase 6
Delivery & Coaching
On approval, the client receives an email notification with a direct link to their roadmap. Admin downloads the PDF for the client's Drive folder. The roadmap is a discussion starter for 1-on-1 coaching sessions — not a prescription.
Ongoing

Human-in-the-loop at every gate. The admin review step is a mandatory manual approval. No roadmap reaches a client without a human reviewing it first. Rejected roadmaps return to generation with admin feedback. The platform automates the heavy lifting; human judgment makes the final call.
