KB Audit Process
AESOP Transformation OS
01
Upload knowledge base documents
Upload your KB source files (.md, .txt, .html, .pdf, .docx) along with the agent's PRD as context. Stored documents can be selected for inclusion per audit.
02
Per-document quality analysis
Each document is scored against four dimensions using a configurable LLM. Images and inline examples are flagged when they contain content not present in the extracted text — a common source of silent knowledge gaps.
Readability
30%
Clarity
30%
Consistency
25%
Completeness
15%
Image & example detection: identifies content locked in images or examples that is absent from extractable text, preventing blind spots in agent retrieval.
03
Cross-document synthesis
Compares all documents as a cohort. Flags contradictory claims (conflicting dates, numbers, or facts), terminology inconsistencies (same concept, different names), and generates prioritized recommendations.
04
Results & recommendations
Overall quality score, risk level assessment, per-document breakdown, conflict detection, and actionable recommendations. Exportable as a downloadable markdown report.
