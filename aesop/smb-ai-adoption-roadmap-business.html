AI ADOPTION ROADMAP

Your AI Adoption Roadmap

A free, personalized plan to operationalize AI in your business — built from how you actually work.

about 10 minutes to complete · personalized to your business · no cost

01

Why this tool exists

Most small and mid-sized businesses know AI matters. But between running the day-to-day, managing a team, and serving customers, there’s no time to figure out where to start. Generic advice says “automate customer support” — it doesn’t know your workflows, your constraints, or what actually makes your business work.

This tool changes that. You spend about 10 minutes describing how work actually gets done — where the friction is, what you’re aiming for, what you can’t compromise on. It builds you a concrete, phased adoption plan that respects your reality.

02

The problem with most AI advice

It’s genericOne-size-fits-all advice ignores your industry, team size, budget, and the three workflows keeping you up at night. Direction without context.
It’s expensiveTraditional AI consulting starts around $10,000 and takes weeks. Most small businesses can’t justify that before knowing whether AI will help.
It won’t say what not to doEvery vendor tells you what to buy. Almost nobody tells you what to skip, what to delay, or what’s too risky for your stage. The roadmap does.
03

How it works

You answer questions about your business; the system builds the roadmap. Four required sections:

01 · your business

Industry, size, customers

How you find and serve customers, and what you wish you had more time for.

02 · your goals

Success in 12 months

Your one-year goal, three-year vision, and what you think AI could do for you.

03 · how work happens

Up to three key workflows

Step by step — the tools, what breaks, and the knowledge living in people’s heads.

04 · your boundaries

Budget, compliance, vetoes

Budget comfort, what can’t go wrong, who decides, and who can veto.

Two optional sections — department structure and current tech projects — sharpen the plan if you fill them in. The core four are enough.

04

What you receive

Executive summary

A clear read on where you stand

What AI opportunities fit your context and the strategic themes that emerged from your answers.

Phased plan

3–5 concrete phases

Specific projects, milestones, and timeframes — each tagged as based on your initiatives or suggested by analysis.

Quick wins

Fast, low-effort momentum

Projects flagged separately so you build confidence before heavier lifts.

Risk awareness

What could go wrong

Per phase: dependencies and what to explicitly not do during it.

05

Every roadmap carries a confidence label

Most AI documents present everything as certain. This one does the opposite: it tells you exactly how much is extrapolated versus grounded in what you told us. Skip a section and the roadmap knows what it’s missing.

ConfidenceScoreMeaning
Well-grounded75–100Solid context behind every recommendation. Act on it with confidence.
Directionally sound50–74Direction is right; sequencing may shift as you fill gaps.
Conversation starter0–49Essentials only — a good starting point, but add detail.

Why this builds trust: the roadmap lists its own gaps — “we don’t know your compliance requirements, so risk mitigations are generic.” You know exactly where to focus follow-up.

06

How The Bradbury Group uses it

TBG shares a link with a small or mid-sized business. The owner spends 10 minutes describing their operation; the system builds a personalized roadmap. A TBG strategist reviews it before release — a human quality check so every roadmap that reaches a client has been vetted by someone who understands adoption in practice.

The SMB gets a credible, actionable plan at zero cost. TBG gets a warm lead with real context. No cold outreach, no generic pitch decks — just a genuinely useful deliverable that starts the conversation from understanding.
