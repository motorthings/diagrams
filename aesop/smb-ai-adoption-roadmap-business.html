The problem with most AI advice

◉

It’s generic

One-size-fits-all advice doesn’t account for your industry, your team size, your budget, or the three workflows that keep you up at night. It’s direction without context.

◎

Traditional AI consulting starts at $10,000 and takes weeks. Most small businesses can’t justify that before they’ve even validated whether AI will help.

Every vendor pitch tells you what to buy. Almost nobody tells you what to skip, what to delay, or what’s too risky for your stage. The roadmap does.

How it works

You answer questions about your business. The system builds a roadmap. Here’s what the questions cover:

Step 1

Your Business

Industry, size, customers, how you find and serve them, and what you wish you had more time for.

Step 2

Your Goals

What success looks like in 12 months, your 3-year vision, and what you think AI could do for your business.

Step 3

How Work Happens

Up to 3 key workflows — step by step — plus the tools you use, what breaks, and the knowledge that lives in people’s heads.

Step 4

Your Boundaries

Budget comfort zone, compliance requirements, what can’t go wrong, who decides, and who can veto.

That’s it. Four required sections. There are two optional ones — your department structure and any current tech projects — that make the roadmap even sharper if you fill them in. But the core four are enough to generate a solid plan.

What you receive

☆

Executive Summary

A clear read on where your business stands, what AI opportunities match your context, and the strategic themes that emerged from your answers.

☰

Phased Plan

3 to 5 concrete phases, each with specific projects, milestones, and timeframes. Every project is tagged as based on your actual initiatives or suggested by the analysis.

↗

Quick Wins

Projects you can implement fast with low effort and high impact — flagged separately so you can build momentum before tackling heavier lifts.

⚠

Risk Awareness

For each phase: what could go wrong, what dependencies exist, and what you should explicitly not do during that phase.
