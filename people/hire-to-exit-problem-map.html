02

Cross-cutting connections

MTA = Separation Agreement
HR lead calls them Mutual Termination Agreements. Abby calls them separation agreements. Same process, same cost, different vocabulary. Any AI work on ER case management or spend analytics needs to treat these as unified.
Quality of Hire drives MTA volume
Chad: quality of hire is unmeasured. HR lead: no way to course-correct poor performers. Abby: processes ~100 exits/year. The volume Abby handles is a downstream consequence of gaps Chad and HR lead own.
PvP and L&D overlap
HR lead’s PvP initiatives are “very similar” to Hannah’s L&D work. Manager enablement spans both teams. Solution architecture must account for this overlap or risk duplication.
Cost flows downhill
Each bad hire costs: recruiter time + ramp time (Chad), manager time + HR cycles + lost productivity (HR lead), legal time + severance package + external counsel (Abby). The total cost is invisible because no one tracks the full lifecycle.
