AESOP Transformation OS

A platform that helps organizations move from “we know we should use AI” to actually running governed, trusted AI agents across the business.

What it does

Every organization hits the same wall with AI.
You build a few agents. They work. Then you build more. Suddenly you have twenty agents, no idea which ones are safe, no consistent way to evaluate them, and no process for keeping them current. The person who built the marketing agent leaves, and nobody knows how it works.
AESOP solves this. It's a single system that takes an AI agent from idea to production — with governance, evaluation, and maintenance built in from day one, not bolted on after something breaks.

How it works — in plain terms

1
Understand your organization first
Before anyone builds anything, AESOP captures how your organization actually works — who decides what, where work gets stuck, what your real priorities are. This isn't a form. It's the foundation everything else is built on, and it stays current as things change.
2
Define the project and agree on scope
Every AI initiative is wrapped in a project container that tracks all work from start to finish. Before anyone writes a line, a Working Agreement spells out exactly what will be delivered, who is responsible, and how fast people need to respond. No scope creep, no orphaned projects.
3
Validate the problem before building
A structured interview asks: Who has this problem? How often? What do they do today? It produces a straightforward GO / NO-GO verdict. If the problem isn't real, frequent, and costly, AESOP tells you to stop before you waste months building something nobody needs.
4
Design the right solution
AESOP generates a product requirements document from the discovery interview and scores which AI platform is the right fit for this specific problem — not just defaulting to the tool you already have. It flags gaps and infeasible requirements before a single line of code is written.
5
Build the agent, with a human checkpoint
A six-phase build pipeline writes the agent's instructions. A human must approve before the build proceeds past the gap analysis phase — this is not a fully automated black box. The pipeline outputs not just instructions, but a clear explanation of what will change, what could go wrong, and what second-order effects to watch for.
6
Evaluate before you deploy
Seven different ways of testing the agent, not just one. Beyond checking if it works, AESOP tests for safety, bias, and edge cases. If safety or bias scores fall below 75%, the agent cannot ship — period. This is a hard rule, not a suggestion.
7
Give the organization what it needs to adopt the agent
A technically sound agent that nobody uses is a failure. AESOP generates four documents from data it already collected: who does what (RACI), what stakeholders need to know (Tiger Team Brief), how to maintain it, and how to roll it out in phases.
8
Monitor and improve continuously
Once agents are live, AESOP tracks how they're performing, what they cost, and whether they're drifting from their original quality. When scores drop or something breaks, the Repair workflow analyzes what went wrong and rewrites instructions — closing the loop between real-world feedback and improvement.
