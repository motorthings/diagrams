Delegation Readiness

An honest assessment of whether your organization can responsibly scale work to autonomous agents — and the action items to close the gaps.

01

Why readiness

The hard part of scaling autonomy is rarely model intelligence — it is permission and coordination. Delegated systems accelerate the consequences of whatever organizational ambiguity already exists. Delegation Readiness judges whether the conditions for delegation are actually in place; if not, it names the gaps and the work to close them. It is allowed to say “not ready.” A green assessment nobody can fail is worthless — discomfort is information.

Principle: the framework is grounded in the Agentic Operating Model — make stopping cheap first, then widen authority. Readiness is not a one-time gate; it is a loop you run as the portfolio grows.

04

The five dimensions

D1Authority &
Decision Rights
Is it clear who can act, override, and pause? Is the stop button owned, and is intervention a designed, low-cost action rather than an act of social defiance?
D2Coordination &
Dependencies
Are handoffs, upstream/downstream dependencies, and exception paths explicit? Will speeding one step flood a downstream team?
D3Tacit Knowledge &
Human Repair
Does the org understand the “tolerated vagueness” people currently absorb, and account for the expertise being displaced — the translation debt?
D4Exposure &
Commitment Posture
Is risk appetite explicit? Does the org grasp the commitments and P&L changes that shipping autonomous agents creates?
D5Portfolio
Coherence
Is the agent portfolio coordinated — clear ownership, departments, prioritization — rather than fragmented parallel experimentation?

How the verdict is decided: Ready overall strong (roughly 75+) with no critical gaps in authority or coordination. Conditional workable, but with named conditions that must be met before widening autonomy. Not ready a critical gap exists — especially in authority or coordination — or context is too thin to judge.

05

The readiness loop

1Capture
context
Add organization context entries — authority maps, coordination and dependencies, tacit knowledge, exposure posture, systemic blockers.
2Generate
Run the assessment. Read the verdict and the dimension rationales honestly — the uncomfortable scores are the useful ones.
3Close gaps
Assign action items by owner role. Promote the substantial ones into the portfolio as backlog projects so they get tracked and delivered.
4Re-assess
Re-generate after the work lands. The new version should show the verdict and scores moving — evidence the org is genuinely more ready.
