Waifinder Assistant

Waifinder answers the questions that keep aspiring AI consultants stuck: how to position yourself, what to charge, how to find clients, and what to learn next. It delivers the kind of nuanced, experience-based guidance you’d normally pay a coach for — built from real practitioner knowledge, not generic career advice.

01

How It Works

01Describe where
you are
Your domain expertise, your current AI skills, what kind of work you want to do. Waifinder doesn’t assume you’re starting from zero — it meets you where you are.
02Queries three
knowledge layers
Practitioner wisdom from AI BuildLab transcripts and interviews. Academic frameworks with confidence ratings. Tactical playbooks with specific numbers and templates. All cross-referenced.
03Synthesized
answer
Not a list of links. A coherent response that combines what practitioners actually did, what the research says, and what specific steps you can take — calibrated to your situation.
04Act, then
come back
As your practice develops, the questions change. Waifinder stays useful across the full arc — from “how do I start?” to “how do I scale?”
Knowledge base: Six interconnected documents — career guidance, research guide, tactical playbook, common mistakes, case studies, and client acquisition. Built from 100+ hours of AI BuildLab transcripts, practitioner interviews, and 100+ cited academic and industry sources. Every research claim includes a confidence rating.
