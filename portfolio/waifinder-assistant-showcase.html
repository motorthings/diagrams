Waifinder Assistant

AI-powered career guidance for professionals transitioning into AI consulting

Waifinder answers the questions that keep aspiring AI consultants stuck: how to position yourself, what to charge, how to find clients, and what to learn next. It delivers the kind of nuanced, experience-based guidance you'd normally pay a coach for — built from real practitioner knowledge, not generic career advice.

How It Works

1

You describe where you are

Your domain expertise, your current AI skills, what kind of work you want to do. Waifinder doesn't assume you're starting from zero — it meets you where you are.

2

It queries across three knowledge layers

Practitioner wisdom from AI BuildLab transcripts and interviews. Academic frameworks with confidence ratings. Tactical playbooks with specific numbers and templates. All cross-referenced.

3

You get a synthesized answer

Not a list of links. A coherent response that combines what practitioners actually did, what the research says, and what specific steps you can take — calibrated to your situation.

4

You act, then come back

As your practice develops, the questions change. Waifinder stays useful across the full arc — from "how do I start?" to "how do I scale?"

Knowledge base: Six interconnected documents — career guidance, research guide, tactical playbook, common mistakes, case studies, and client acquisition. Built from 100+ hours of AI BuildLab transcripts, practitioner interviews, and 100+ cited academic and industry sources. Every research claim includes a confidence rating.

What You Get

A clear positioning

Lead with your domain expertise, layer AI on top. Guidance on how to describe what you do so clients understand the value — not a vague "AI consultant" label.

Real pricing guidance

Specific numbers, retainer structures, value-based vs. hourly trade-offs. Built from what practitioners actually charge — not theoretical rate cards.

A client acquisition method

Discovery frameworks, the "show up with a mockup" approach, objection handling. How to find and close your first clients without feeling like a salesperson.

A skill progression path

What to learn next to increase your rates. From tool proficiency to workflow design to strategic advisory — with realistic timelines and milestones.

Mistakes to avoid

Real failure patterns catalogued from practitioner experience. Pricing too low, scoping too broad, skipping the discovery phase — know the traps before you step in them.

A scalable operating model

When to hire, how to build repeatable methodologies, when to productize vs. stay custom. The playbook for growing from solo practice to small firm.
