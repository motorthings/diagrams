Waifinder Assistant

AI-powered career guidance for professionals transitioning into AI consulting

Waifinder is an AI assistant that helps professionals navigate the transition into AI consulting — not with generic career advice, but with practitioner-sourced knowledge, research-backed frameworks, and tactical playbooks built from real experience.

It delivers the kind of nuanced, experience-based guidance typically only available through expensive coaching or mentorship programs.

What Waifinder Guides On

Career Positioning

How to leverage your existing domain expertise with AI skills. Don't call yourself an "AI consultant" — lead with what you know, layer AI on top.

Pricing & Business Models

Real pricing examples, retainer structures, value-based vs. hourly trade-offs. Specific numbers from practitioners, not theoretical advice.

Client Acquisition

Discovery frameworks, the "show up with a mockup" approach, objection handling, and how to find and close your first clients.

Skill Development

Progression paths — what to learn next to increase your rates. From tool proficiency to workflow design to strategic advisory.

Mistake Prevention

Real failure patterns and warning signs. Common mistakes catalogued from practitioner experience with strategies to avoid them.

Scaling Your Practice

From solo practitioner to small firm. When to hire, how to build repeatable methodologies, and when to productize vs. stay custom.
