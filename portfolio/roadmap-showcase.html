In Your Own Words

You told us specific things about your business. Here are your words — and exactly how the roadmap responds to each one. This is how we know the map is yours, not a template.

You Said
“Biggest obstacle: Owner is the bottleneck. Every quote, schedule, parts order, and quality check depends on one person. The business is the owner.”
Our Response
Every phase systematically removes owner dependencies. By Phase 3, the business can operate for two weeks without the owner present.
You Said
“Customer calls → office manager triages → owner inspects every boat → writes quote by hand → orders parts → schedules techs on whiteboard → techs use paper checklists → owner final inspection → office manager invoices. Average job 3-5 days but scheduling adds 2-3 days.”
Our Response
Phase 1 replaces paper with digital work tracking. Nothing gets lost, and every job is searchable. Phase 2 deploys AI-assisted scheduling. The whiteboard becomes a backup, not the system of record. Owner time on scheduling drops 60%+.
You Said
“Parts sourcing during emergencies is chaos. Insurance quotes miss 48-hour SLA 30% of time. Emergency jobs displace scheduled work.”
Our Response
Phase 1 builds a supplier price database. Phase 2 automates purchase orders and emergency triage. Parts stop arriving wrong or late. The owner retains final authority on crew dispatch.
You Said
“Top goal: Increase annual job capacity from ~200 to ~350 without adding more than 3 technicians. Success definition: Owner under 5 hours/week on scheduling and quoting. Tech utilization above 85%.”
Our Response
Phase 1 templates the quoting logic. What took hours becomes a 15-minute review. Margins become consistent. By Phase 3, AI handles intake-to-quote — owner reviews and approves in one click.

Risk Mitigations

Named risks. Specific mitigations.

HIGHOwner resistance to documenting proprietary quoting knowledge built over 30 years

Frame as succession planning and business value protection. Start with simple scenarios and build complexity gradually. Show direct link to owner time reduction.

HIGHOwner / Master Tech remains single point of failure for critical decisions

Cross-train Linda Chen on emergency triage decisions. Document hurricane protocols. Create decision delegation matrix for routine approvals. Build backup authority before AI deployment.

HIGHAI quote accuracy insufficient for insurance work requiring 48-hour SLA

Start with standard repair quotes, not insurance. Build accuracy over 3 months before enabling insurance workflow. Maintain manual override for complex jobs.

MEDException Inflation — automated status updates create more customer questions than they answer

Size customer service capacity before scaling. Add quality gates between automated updates and customer delivery. Measure inquiry volume as success metric, not just deflection rate.

MEDAdmin Moved to Seams — quoting automation creates more exception handling for office manager

Map full workflow end-to-end before automating. Count hidden repair work as project cost. Train Linda Chen on quote review and exception handling. Measure coordination overhead weekly.
