Personal operating rule

The Offloading Line

AI is best at what you can already do but find tedious. You keep what only you can do. The line isn’t between tasks — it’s between the part of a task where thinking happens and the part where producing happens.

01

Judgment versus production

AI produces. It drafts, enumerates, formats, remembers, retrieves, generates the hundred mediocre versions. That’s production, and it all goes to the machine. Judgment is the part where you decide what matters, what’s true, what to do, what’s good enough.

Judgment is quiet. Drafting feels like work, so it’s easy to hand over. Deciding doesn’t feel like work, so it’s easy to keep. But deciding is the whole job — and it’s also the part that’s easiest to sneak off to the machine without noticing.

You keep · judgment
Deciding what matters. What’s true. What to do. What ships. Every time the AI makes that call for you, you’ve paid for convenience with the one thing you’re trying to keep.
AI gets · production
Drafting, enumerating, formatting, remembering, retrieving, generating versions. The throughput. Anything you can already do but find tedious is a candidate to hand over.
02

Same idea, three angles

First / lastYou take both ends
Face the blank page and form your own bad hypothesis first — starting from nothing is where thinking happens. Then AI runs the messy middle. Then you take the last pass: review, veto, decide.
Breadth / depthAI is wide, you’re deep
It surfaces fifty options. You pick one. It’s good at width, bad at conviction, and conviction is built only by making the call and owning it.
Loop / artifactThe loop is the skill
The artifact is AI’s. The loop is yours: notice something’s off, figure out why, fix it. That notice-to-diagnosis-to-fix cycle is cognition. Keep it even when you outsource the output.
03

One question, self-checking

Would I still be able to do this cold if the AI disappeared tomorrow?

Ask it once per skill you care about. If the answer is no, you haven’t augmented the skill — you’ve replaced it. That’s the whole border, and it polices itself.

04

Where the leak actually is

None of these announce themselves. You cross them by drift, not decision — and the cost is paid in capability you don’t miss until you need it and it’s gone.

1The prompt itself
Framing the question is half the thinking. Ask vaguely and let it frame, and you’ve offloaded judgment wearing a costume. Did you specify the problem, or ask “what should I do about X?”
2Between answer and accept
“Looks right” is not review. If you can’t catch a subtle wrongness, you no longer have the skill — you have the AI’s skill by proxy. The seam is whether you can tell when it’s quietly off.
3Between options and choice
It surfaces fifty paths. Take its “I’d recommend” without a reason of your own and you’ve made a decision you didn’t make. Pick one for a reason you can state.
4Between draft and edit
Editing is thinking. Reworking a sentence, cutting a paragraph, reordering the argument — that’s where your taste operates. Ship the draft “good enough” and you’ve ceded the last pass. Do you touch it after it comes back?
5Between knowing and remembering
Ask it “what did I decide about X” and the decision migrates from your head to the log. The machine can hold the data. Keep the why — the reasoning, the tradeoffs you chose.
6Between doing and delegating
The “I could do this myself but it’s faster if...” moment. Faster is real. But every time you take faster on a skill you said you’d keep, the border moves.
7The calibration seam
Predict before you ask. Skip the guess and you never learn how often you’re wrong and how often it’s wrong. You lose the ability to tell them apart, and that’s the whole game.
05

Holding the line

Predict firstCommit to a guess
State your answer, then check the AI’s. Calibration only survives if you put a stake in the ground before you look.
Do it coldOn a schedule
The rule isn’t “never let AI do X.” It’s “make sure you still do X unassisted, sometimes.” Pick three skills you’d refuse to lose and do them by hand once a week, badly if you have to.
No-AI zoneWhere deciding is the skill
Your thesis. Your values. The hard conversation. The final call. Not because AI can’t help — because the point is that you make them.

Hold the ends — the framing, the choice, the edit, the reason. The machine gets the middle.
