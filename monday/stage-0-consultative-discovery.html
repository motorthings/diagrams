Stark Industries PMO · from an email to an architecture

FOUNDATION

How I processed Pepper's request. The assignment is a PMO Director's email asking for intake, SLA tracking, and a dashboard. The evaluation isn't about monday.com expertise — it's about architectural instincts, consultative thinking, and AI as a working tool. This diagram traces the chain from what Pepper said → what I heard → what I built → what I deliberately cut.

What Pepper Said
Explicit requirements from the PMO Director
"Requests are vague — I need a standardized intake process"
Problem #1: Inconsistent submission quality
"We need SLA tracking — requests reviewed within 5 days"
Problem #2: No accountability on review timelines
"An executive dashboard so leadership can see portfolio health"
Problem #3: Manual spreadsheet-to-PowerPoint grind
"I have 3 PMs who'd manage from a portfolio view"
Constraint: 3-person PM team, strategic oversight needed
What I Heard
The problems she didn't name but implied
No triage layer exists IMPLICIT
Someone has to read every request and decide: is this complete? Is this actually a project? Before the 5-day clock even starts.
SLA needs escalation, not just measurement IMPLICIT
A 5-day SLA that silently expires isn't tracking — it's a stopwatch. Someone needs to be notified when the clock is running out.
Exec view ≠ PM view IMPLICIT
Pepper manually builds PowerPoints because the raw data isn't structured for leadership consumption. The two views need different boards.
Request quality is the root cause IMPLICIT
Vague requests downstream create all her problems. Fix intake, and everything downstream improves. This is where AI earns its place.

What We Built
The LEAN MVP — traceable to assignment text
8 form fields → AI Summary + Completeness Formula
AI handles fuzzy (summarize vague requests), formula handles deterministic (field completeness check). Two distinct layers.
4 automations across Intake + Portfolio
Completeness routing, SLA breach notification, PM assignment, Execution Board creation on approval. End-to-end automated.
Connect Boards + mirror roll-up
Status and Due Date roll up from Execution → Portfolio. PMs see trajectory without clicking through. Zero manual sync.
4 dashboard widgets — all live, no manual work
Portfolio Health, SLA Compliance, Active Projects, Department Load. Pepper never opens PowerPoint again.
